End-to-end Gym environments for upper-limb rehabilitation. They model patient–robot interaction dynamics and use clinically meaningful reward structures, so that assistive movements can be trained in a controlled way.

I evaluated SAC on them for stability, safety and adaptability. This work is part of NeuRehab, published in *Neuromorphic Computing and Engineering*.

[Code on GitHub](https://github.com/Chainesh/rehabrl)
